# Where the golden-set plans come from, and whether we may use them

A commercial product cannot test on non-commercial data. Two of the best floorplan
datasets are unusable here for exactly that reason, so this file records provenance
rather than leaving it to be rediscovered.

|---|---|---|---|

## Datasets evaluated and REJECTED on licence

| Dataset | Size | Licence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CubiCasa5K](https://zenodo.org/record/2613548) | 5,000 real CAD plans, 80+ categories, SVG | **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** | ❌ Non-commercial. The richest option and we cannot touch it |
| [FloorPlanCAD](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Voxel51/FloorPlanCAD) | 15,663 CAD drawings, SVG + PNG | **CC BY-NC 4.0** | ❌ Non-commercial |
| [RPLAN](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~fuxm/projects/DeepLayout/) | 80,788 rasterised plans | Research request required | ❌ Residential only, and permission-gated |

## Evaluated and usable, but not a fit

- [ResPlan](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14006v1) — 17,000 plans, permissive licence, but
  **residential only** (apartments, villas, townhouses). This product is hospitality.
- [MLSTRUCT-FP](https://pypi.org/project/MLStructFP) — MIT, ~954 plans with wall
  annotations. Usable, generic building stock rather than hospitality.
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Floor_plans_of_hotels) —
  271 hotel plans, overwhelmingly public domain. Mostly early-20th-century and mostly
  under 1000px; `blackstone_hotel_1910.png` came from here. The 1904 Hotel Astor sheet
  was tried and the Intake Agent correctly rejected it as `low_res`, which is the
  product working, not a failure.

## The gap only real plans can close

**No open Arabic or MENA floorplan dataset exists.** Arabic *reports* are tested end to
end; Arabic *plan reading* has never been tested at all, and it is the stated launch
differentiator. [Archnet](https://www.archnet.org) (MIT) is the best archive of
Islamic-world architecture but is photographic and scholarly rather than a labelled plan
set. Closing this needs real plans from the founder's network — ideally anonymised
client drawings with Arabic room labels.
